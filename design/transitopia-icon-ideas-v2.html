Ten new directions built around both halves of Transitopia: a connected transit system and an optimistic place to live. Trains, green neighborhoods, welcoming stations, and a brighter urban future.

Round 02 explores transit + utopia, rather than standalone train or letter symbols. Each concept separates foreground and background and includes a monochrome treatment.
