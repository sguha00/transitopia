Ten adaptive icon directions for Transitopia. A recognizable mark for the next train, your station, and the journey beyond.

Each concept separates foreground and background and includes a monochrome treatment. The large icon uses your selected mask; the smaller views test a circle, a 48px silhouette, and themed color. These are design previews; the app keeps its current icon until you choose a direction.
My shortlist: 01 Track T for the clearest brand, 03 Connected T for a transit network identity, and 06 Interchange for a distinctive standalone symbol.

Preview construction: 108-unit layers, a 72-unit mask window, and a central 66-unit circular safe area. These simulations help compare concepts; final Android launcher behavior still needs on-device verification. Android adaptive icon guidance ↗
